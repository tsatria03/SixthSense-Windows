**Don't write down each release the dev makes.** No note in `aidocks/`, and no line in `README.md`, says which versions went out, when, with how many changelog entries, or with which zip, tag or "Release" commit. That includes the credits: they say what someone did for the game, not that they made release x.

**Why:** The dev, 2026-09-25: "Please stop recording every single release I make into your docks, including readme.md. People do not need to see that I made x release." Until then every release had been added to [[feedback_changelog]], [[project_release_tooling_plan]], `MEMORY.md` and the README's credits; those records were taken out the same day.

**How to apply:**
- When the dev says they released, check GitHub if asked, and answer in chat. Don't offer to record it.
- The rules about releases stay: the 5 and 100 entry limits and the "Release anyway?" question ([[feedback_changelog]]), and how the releaser works ([[project_release_tooling_plan]]). What goes is the per-release history.
- Version numbers used only as examples of the format, such as `26.09.23-1` in the README's releaser steps, are fine.
- The changelog's own version headings are the releaser's work and stay as they are.
